## One-company cost accounting and scoreboard

Default private ledger: Pictures/SDPP/Marketing/Google Ads/SDPP-Jacksonville-Test.sqlite. This is an experiment reporting ledger, not a replacement accounting company or the customer treatment workbook. Import actual expense allocations from SDPP records using stable IDs. The legal entity remains SDPP for every row. Campaign is JAX_PHC, JAX_SOURCING or blank for other SDPP marketing. Mark marketing=1 only for costs classified internally as marketing/business development; this flag is not a tax-deductibility decision.

Categories cover Google Ads, website and landing-page development, call tracking, software, travel, sourcing research, partner costs, freight research, photography/content, laboratory/diagnostics, market development and miscellaneous. Split shared actual costs once using documented allocation percentages and child IDs tied to a single source invoice; never duplicate the full invoice in both campaigns. Source ledger and receipt references go in note. No amounts have been fabricated or posted as actual expense.

Traffic imports carry actual Google Ads spend and deduplicated clicks/calls/forms. Expense Google Ads rows reconcile to traffic spend; reports show the difference, and do not add both. Total test expense = actual ad spend + non-ad test expenses. Ad CPQL = ad spend / first-qualified leads. Fully loaded CPQL = total test expense / qualified leads. Booked cost = ad spend / first booked opportunities. Marketing CAC = ad + non-ad marketing expense / new won transactions. ROAS = closed revenue / ad spend. Fulfillment cost includes palm purchase, freight, crane, installation and direct fulfillment fees not already in test expenses; contribution deducts fulfillment and test expense once. Distinguish closed transactions from unique customers when repeat customers recur.

Weekly report --start YYYY-MM-DD --days 7 produces both HTML and JSON, individually and combined. It includes expense, clicks, calls, forms, qualified/booked/quoted/won counts, revenue, all ratios, pipeline, and structured weekly review fields. Flow metrics use first recorded stage dates, including older-lead outcomes. Cohort fields identify leads acquired within the window and their outcomes to date. Ratios are period efficiency signals, not perfect cohort CAC. Current open quoted pipeline is a snapshot, not a reconstructed historical pipeline. Null ratios mean a zero denominator.

The review JSON records week, campaign, best_location, best_theme, best_asset, worst_waste, recommendation. Use available reports and qualified-lead evidence; report insufficient data rather than declare a winner from one click. Combined Jacksonville uses the same structure. Record SDPP non-JAX marketing expenses to obtain total recorded SDPP marketing and JAX share; incomplete imports mean incomplete company totals.

## Thirty-day decision

Set Day 1 to the actual first advertising day, not the build date. report --start DAY1 --days 30 adds cohort-based directional signal checks. PHC: 5+ qualified leads, 3+ booked opportunities, 2+ paying or evidenced viable opportunities, recurring demand and specialist-value evidence. Record viable_opportunity with viability_evidence, recurring_interest with recurring_evidence, and values_specialist with specialist_evidence. These are evidence flags, not revenue. Sourcing: 3+ qualified inquiries and one serious quote request, with quote_requested and quote_request_evidence; inspect species/size/budget/installation learning. Small-sample counts do not establish profitability.

Before launch, owner selects acceptable fully loaded CPQL/CAC and required contribution using real pricing, delivery scope, close rate and margin. Do not invent a universal target. Maximum acquisition cost per paying job should fit expected contribution before acquisition, and allowable CPQL should reflect observed close probability and risk. Sourcing uses landed cost, not sale value alone. Pause a term after it exceeds its pre-agreed learning budget without a qualified inquiry; review low volume separately from poor lead quality. Record one next-week change per campaign, then measure it.

Week 1: validate delivery, calls and attribution; review terms daily for waste. Week 2: compare qualified issue/location data, not raw lead counts. Week 3: resolve real quotes and partner economics; test one promising theme. Day 30: continue, narrow, revise or stop each offer independently. Scaling requires repeatable qualified demand, positive feasible contribution and a verified delivery path. The initial counts are directional, not permanent KPIs.
