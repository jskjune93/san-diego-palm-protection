## Cannibalization boundaries

- `sapw.html` answers informational pest questions; the SAPW treatment route sells the treatment service.
- `palm-stewardship-plans.html` covers general preventive palm treatment; the SAPW treatment route covers the specific pest.
- `palm-records-monitoring-verification.html` explains deliverables and routes inquiries; the managed-property route owns commercial portfolio-management searches.
- `quarterly-palm-care-san-diego.html` owns recurring stewardship and preservation, not one-time assessment or pest-specific treatment.
- Location pages add genuine local context and point to canonical service pages; they do not define separate service architectures.
