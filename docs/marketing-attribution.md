# SDPP inquiry attribution

Updated October 18, 2026.

The delivered inquiry email contains an inquiry reference, the customer's optional discovery source and detail, and available browser-reported first/latest visit evidence. It retains landing paths, referring hostnames, campaign source/medium/name and Google click identifiers. Full referring URLs, search queries and arbitrary URL parameters are not retained. Evidence lasts within the browser tab for up to 30 days; blocked storage and missing referrers do not prevent submission. This does not reconstruct earlier leads.

## Following an inquiry through payment

Keep the inquiry reference on the appointment and invoice record. For phone or text inquiries, record the date and customer in the normal client record and ask: “How did you find me? If it was Google, do you remember a Sponsored result, Maps, or a regular search result?” Preserve “not sure” when appropriate.

Record these fields in the existing client record: inquiry reference, inquiry date, reported source, observed source evidence, assessment date, booked service, invoice number, amount invoiced, amount paid, payment date, and direct service costs. A booked program and collected cash are separate outcomes. Do not count the full annual program price as collected cash after one quarterly payment.

Google Search selected by a customer does not establish organic attribution. A Google click identifier supports paid-click evidence; Google Ads attribution and conversion reports remain authoritative for the ad platform. AI self-reports and AI referral hostnames are separate evidence. Missing referrers mean unknown/direct, not confirmed organic.

The website does not automatically sync appointments, invoices, payments, or calls into Google Ads. Do not upload customer contact data or record a phone-button click as a completed lead. The primary website conversion still requires successful server-confirmed form delivery. A non-personal inquiry reference deduplicates the conversion; failed retries reuse the delivery key when the form has not changed.

## Review requests

Ask every completed-service customer neutrally, without incentives or screening for satisfaction. Draft for use after a completed visit:

“Thanks for having me out to care for your palm. If you would like to share your experience, an honest Google review helps other local owners find SDPP. You can find San Diego Palm Protection on Google Maps and choose Write a review. Thank you, John.”

This is an unsent template. No automated review messages are configured.
