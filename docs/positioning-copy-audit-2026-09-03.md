# Positioning and primary-page copy audit — 2026-09-03

## Standard applied

The review used “polished presentation plus verifiable real-world proof” as the governing standard. SDPP remains an owner-operated palm-stewardship and preservation company for mature and high-value palms. Copy must describe direct assessment, photography, documentation, preventive treatment within licensed scope, monitoring, recovery support, and coordination without implying employees, equipment, nursery inventory, relocation crews, or work performed by others.

## Primary-page disposition

- Homepage: replaced the generic portfolio-first hero with a direct description of owner-led field work; added a documented-field-work opening and a four-step explanation of what happens after contact. Retained the commercial portfolio position, four stewardship pillars, two audience paths, Field Work links, and baseline CTA.
- About: retained the owner story, credentials, Old Escondido origin, and direct-involvement language. Replaced institutional/corporate headings and fear-heavy phrasing with plainspoken early-documentation and continuity explanations. Removed decorative third-party university marks.
- Services: retained the concise assessment, preventive treatment, fertilization/recovery, monitoring, reporting, and decline-response overview plus the two working inquiry paths.
- Commercial and managed properties: retained the Palm Portfolio Baseline, Annual Palm Stewardship Program, defined deliverables, contractor boundaries, and representative documentation example.
- Residential assessment: retained the photo-based on-site assessment, written findings, comparison process, limitations, and direct residential CTA.
- Palm stewardship and prevention: retained factual treatment availability, assessment-first decisions, monitoring, diagnostic limits, and no-guarantee language.
- Recurring stewardship: retained “Palm stewardship and preservation,” fertilization, irrigation review, preventive protection, monitoring, and the variable visit interval.
- Premium sourcing and planting: replaced the generic shared service template with sourcing guidance, re-homing/transplant review, planting coordination, establishment baselines, and continuing transplant-health assessment. Added an explicit statement that SDPP does not own nursery inventory, trucks, cranes, or relocation crews and does not assume supplier or contractor responsibilities.
- Canary Island date palm page: retained species-specific crown observation, assessment, treatment, monitoring, and evidence limits.
- SAPW pages: retained factual urgency, prevention, documented local activity, treatment availability, and the observation-versus-diagnosis boundary.
- Field Work: retained the sanitized documentation example and added four visible approved field records with accurate captions and privacy/client-status limits.
- Palm Journal and Documented Loss: retained established URLs, dated editorial records, uncertainty language, and confirmed-outcome boundaries.

## Consolidation decisions

No primary route was removed. Existing pages were already comparatively concise, and broad deletion would have weakened distinct search and visitor intent. Changes focused on the first decision-making content, unsupported visual authority, sourcing boundaries, and repetitive generic copy. Forms, analytics hooks, metadata, canonical URLs, navigation, credentials, redirects, and production integrations were preserved.
