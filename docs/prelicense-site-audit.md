## Compliance Delta: Monitoring, Inspection, Diagnosis, and Future Treatment

After California and San Diego County licensing research clarified the operating boundary, the site was re-audited for softer ambiguity around monitoring, inspections, assessments, recommendations, consultations, diagnosis, and future or contingent treatment language.

| File | Location | Existing wording or description | Classification | Risk/reason | Action taken | Final disposition |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| `scripts/validate_prelicense_compliance.py` | rules/self-tests | No explicit checks for monitoring-as-service, professional inspections, treatment waitlists, post-licensing reservations, or photo-based treatment recommendations | E | Future copy could pass the older validator while implying a current or contingent regulated service. | Added hard-block rules and fixture tests for those patterns. | Validator now blocks the clarified boundary. |
| `site-config/business_status.json` | explanation | Future activation language did not name the QAL, Pest Control Business License, financial responsibility, and county registration sequence. | E | Could imply an exam pass or QAL alone is enough to activate services. | Expanded explanation to require explicit owner-controlled activation after all listed prerequisites. | Prelicense mode unchanged. |
| `docs/prelicense-compliance.md` | purpose/configuration | Documentation did not explicitly state that "palm monitoring" should not be presented as a paid service. | E | Monitoring could be misread as a commercial safe harbor. | Added the observation/photography/historical-comparison lane and blocked monitoring-as-service, inspections, prescriptions, and contingent treatment offers. | Documented. |
| public root pages | CTAs and body copy | "first look", "inspection", "assessment", "recommendations", and "consultation" appeared in several high-intent contexts. | E | Could imply professional inspection, diagnosis, or site-specific recommendations. | Reframed to educational photo review, photographic condition review, visible-condition records, documentation notes, and licensed-provider questions. | Public CTAs remain documentation-oriented. |
| `palm-stewardship-plans.html` | stewardship sections | Treatment recommendations, care recommendations, and inspection-oriented wording. | E | Could imply site-specific treatment advice or professional inspection. | Reframed as SAPW-aware education, photographic records, historical comparison, seasonal documentation notes, and referral questions. | Stewardship page remains educational and premium without offering disabled services. |
| `cidp-risk-checklist.html` | disclaimer | Disclaimer contained "pesticide recommendation" and "treatment education" wording. | B/E | The sentence was negative, but awkward and easy to misread. | Reworded to "not a diagnosis, pesticide advice, treatment advice, or guarantee." | Clear educational disclaimer. |
| Palm Journal source/generated pages | article CTAs | Generated pages used "first look" and "diagnosis" phrasing. | E/B | CTA language could imply a service; diagnosis references are generally disclaimers. | Builder now uses educational photo review and "unsupported diagnosis" language. | Journal architecture preserved. |

No contingent treatment solicitation, waitlist, deposit, prebooking, future-treatment offer, site-specific pesticide prescription, or SDPP professional-inspection offer remains in public files.
