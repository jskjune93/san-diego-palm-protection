A usable history for every palm

A folder of unrelated photographs is not a property record. SDPP creates consistent palm identifiers, dated images, visible-condition observations, and follow-up notes so owners and decision-makers can see what changed and carry that history forward.

Who it serves

Homeowners, HOAs, property managers, commercial properties, organizations, civic and neighborhood initiatives, and multi-property owners.

What it records

Identity, location, visible condition, chronology, supplied contractor documents, removal or replacement outcomes, and the next useful documentation date.

What it does not claim

These records do not provide a pest diagnosis, arborist certification, engineering review, code determination, insurance verification, or pesticide recommendation.

Work & Outcome Verification

SDPP can create an owner-side record before and after work performed by an outside contractor: pre-work photographs, the client-supplied scope, post-work photographs, observable completion notes, unresolved discrepancies, and the updated palm history.

Scope boundary: Verification documents observable conditions and apparent completion. It is not certification of technical correctness, code compliance, arboricultural standards, pesticide work, or contractor performance beyond SDPP's qualifications.

Removal & Replacement Records

A documented loss record may preserve observed decline, earlier photographs, correspondence supplied by the owner, contractor information supplied by the client, the date removal was documented, final-condition photographs, and replacement history.

SDPP can organize questions and records and coordinate communication with qualified outside contractors. SDPP does not currently perform palm removal, pruning, stump grinding, planting, or installation.

HOA & Portfolio Documentation

Consistent identifiers and dated records preserve continuity across board, manager, vendor, and ownership changes. The resulting material can support priority discussions and be shared with arborists, licensed providers, insurers, contractors, residents, and other decision-makers.

  • Multi-property inventories and repeat monitoring
  • Board-ready summaries and priority lists
  • Contractor scope and apparent-completion records
  • Removal and replacement history

SDPP does not replace an ISA Certified Arborist, pest-control professional, engineer, insurer, or municipal tree assessor.

Organizational & Urban-Forest Support

Localized inventories, repeatable photo baselines, neighborhood context, documented losses, community-submitted observations, and public educational materials can add useful palm-specific evidence to broader planning conversations.

SDPP documentation can supplement, but does not replace, a formal municipal tree inventory, arborist assessment, biological survey, engineering review, or adopted urban-forest management process. No official municipal or urban-forest-plan role is implied.
