About John and SDPP

Canary Island date palms

I take South American palm weevil seriously.

I have dealt with South American palm weevil activity on my own Old Escondido property. That firsthand experience is one reason I pay close attention to crown change and to photographs that show when a change began.

Do not wait for a guess to become an emergency

A photograph cannot diagnose a pest. It can show whether the crown is changing and give a licensed treatment provider something useful to review.
