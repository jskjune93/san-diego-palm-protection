South American Palm Weevil Activity in Escondido and Poway

These photos show examples of suspected South American Palm Weevil activity and serious palm decline observed around the area. This page is educational and meant to help homeowners recognize warning signs before a valuable palm is lost.

Not every declining palm has South American Palm Weevil, but visible crown thinning, collapse, trunk damage, abnormal browning, wet areas, or sudden decline are all reasons to take a closer look.

Not every declining palm has South American Palm Weevil. Irrigation issues, disease, nutrient problems, poor pruning, or other pests can also cause stress and decline. A proper assessment looks at the whole palm, including the crown, trunk, irrigation, site conditions, and surrounding pest pressure.
