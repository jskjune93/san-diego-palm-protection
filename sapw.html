South American Palm Weevil Activity in Escondido & Poway

These photos are examples of suspected South American Palm Weevil activity observed locally. This page is meant to help homeowners recognize what palm decline can look like before a valuable palm is lost. Once serious crown damage is obvious, decline can move quickly.

Not every struggling palm has SAPW, but visible thinning, crown collapse, sudden browning, holes, oozing, unusual odor, or heavy decline should be taken seriously — especially on high-value palms.

Not every declining palm has South American Palm Weevil. Irrigation issues, disease, nutrient problems, transplant stress, drought stress, or poor pruning can also cause stress and decline. A proper assessment looks at the whole palm, site conditions, crown, trunk, irrigation, and surrounding pest pressure.

Warning Signs Worth Checking

These symptoms do not prove SAPW by themselves, but they are worth investigating early.

Crown Thinning

A shrinking, uneven, or hollow-looking crown can be an early visual concern.

Sudden Browning

Rapid browning, drooping, or collapse near the center of the palm should not be ignored.

Trunk or Crown Damage

Holes, wet-looking areas, frass, odor, or unusual damage can justify a closer assessment.
