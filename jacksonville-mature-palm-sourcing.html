A search for your project

The right specimen starts with the right requirements.

For private estates, landscape contractors, designers, developers, HOAs and commercial properties. Describe a Canary Island date palm, Sylvester palm, other Phoenix palm or another specimen you are trying to find. Alternative species may make the project more practical.

Define the palm

Species, quantity, clear-trunk height, desired overall size, budget and timing. Clear trunk and overall height are different measurements.

Check possible sources

Availability, condition, suitability and price must be confirmed for each candidate. Possible sources may be in California or elsewhere. No palm shown or discussed is reserved inventory.

Review the landed cost

Purchase, digging and preparation, freight, permits or plant-entry requirements, crane access, installation and establishment care all affect feasibility. Local partners and their scope must be confirmed.

Before a quote

Can the palm get to the planting site?

Send a wide view of the installation area and describe road access, overhead lines, gates and crane space. Delivery or installation is not included unless a written quote says so. Plant suitability, inspection requirements, freight and contractor availability are reviewed before a transaction.

SDPP is offering sourcing and coordination inquiries, not advertising a Jacksonville nursery or confirmed local inventory. A search request does not guarantee availability, transplant success or an installation date.
