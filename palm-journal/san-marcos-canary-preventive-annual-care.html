A San Marcos Canary Enters Preventive Care

November 4, 2026 | San Marcos, San Diego County · By John Krause

A September prevention inquiry led to an initial treatment and an annual care plan for one San Marcos Canary Island date palm.

In September, a San Marcos homeowner contacted me about protecting one Canary Island date palm. The palm looked okay to the owner, and they wanted to begin preventive care. On September 26, I completed its initial protection treatment under an annual program.

Start with the palm you want to keep

The owner's question was about prevention. There was no need to turn that question into a claim of infestation. A palm that looks okay to its owner still deserves an individual look and a conversation about what protection and continuing care will involve.

For this palm, the September visit was the start of an annual arrangement. That connects the first completed treatment to the next care discussion, rather than leaving the owner to start over each time they have a question.

Keep the next step clear

My follow-up to the owner identified December 26 as the next treatment due date. A due date helps keep the care interval in view; the actual visit still needs to be arranged. If the palm's appearance changes before then, that is a reason to get in touch rather than wait for the date.

This field note looks back at the September work. It does not establish the palm's condition today. The useful next step is to keep watching the same tree and bring any new observations into the next conversation.
