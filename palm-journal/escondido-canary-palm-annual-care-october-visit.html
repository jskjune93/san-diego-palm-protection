Starting Palm Care: The Visit and the Continuing Plan

November 1, 2026 | Escondido, San Marcos and San Diego County · By John Krause

Three first visits, three care decisions: prevention, an offered plan and the start of annual care.

A first treatment and a continuing care plan are two decisions. I want owners to understand what happened at the visit and what they have actually chosen for the months ahead.

Prevention before visible trouble

In September, a San Marcos homeowner asked about protecting a Canary Island date palm that looked okay to them. I completed its initial treatment on September 26 under an annual program. The next due target was December 26, with the appointment still to arrange. A request for prevention did not establish an infestation.

A completed visit does not imply enrollment

At a separate San Diego County property on October 23, I treated one Canary and took dated photographs. I offered continuing care for the owner to consider. The visit was completed; an annual arrangement had not been selected in that account. Keeping those facts separate helps the owner make a clear decision.

Starting annual care in Escondido

On October 28, I treated one Canary at an Escondido home as the first completed visit under the owners' annual plan. The remaining care and next service were still to arrange at the time of the original November 1 note.

What to settle before the next visit

Ask which palms the plan covers, what work has been completed, when the next treatment is due and who will arrange it. If something changes before that target, call with a whole-palm photograph and a closer view of the area that concerns you. I want the next conversation to build on the last visit.

These three accounts describe different owners' decisions and dated work. They do not establish the palms' condition today or a treatment outcome. The distinction is practical: a care plan should tell an owner what comes next, without assuming a booking or an agreement they have not made.

Discuss annual palm protection if you want a continuing plan for a mature palm.

Service dates: September 26, October 23 and October 28, 2026. Original notes published November 4, October 29 and November 1, respectively. Combined November 10, 2026.
