An Escondido Canary Palm Begins Annual Care

November 1, 2026 | Escondido, San Diego County · By John Krause

One Canary Island date palm treated in Escondido on October 28, with annual care chosen and the next service to arrange.

On October 28, I treated one Canary Island date palm at an Escondido home. The owners chose annual care, with that treatment as the first completed visit under their plan.

One mature palm can be a big part of a home's landscape. It deserves a clear plan for the work ahead and a person the owner can reach when something changes.

Carry the first visit forward

For this property, the next step is to arrange the remaining annual scope and the next visit with the owners. I want them to know what we have agreed to and when I am coming back.

Between visits, a new whole-palm photograph is a useful way to show me a change. Include when you first noticed it and which part of the palm concerns you. That helps me understand the question and discuss whether a closer assessment is needed.

Care for the palm you already have

This October visit is one part of continuing care for an established Canary Island date palm. The work ahead is to keep the owner and the next service connected, with attention to changes between visits.

Have a mature Canary Island date palm in Escondido or North County? Text John a whole-palm photo, your city and palm count to discuss an assessment and preventive care.
