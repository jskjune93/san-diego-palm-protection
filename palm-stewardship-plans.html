When protection may be considered

The recommendation follows the palm and the evidence.

Mature Canary Island date palms, locally significant palms, known pest pressure, visible change, or a prevention objective may warrant assessment. A symptom or photograph alone does not establish a diagnosis or automatically justify treatment.
