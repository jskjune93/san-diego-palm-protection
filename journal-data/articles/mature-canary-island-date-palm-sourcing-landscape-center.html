I’m working with an Oceanside homeowner on replacing a Canary Island date palm. My part is helping find the replacement, compare the actual specimens and coordinate the removal and installation plan with Landscape Center.

On October 24, I visited Landscape Center and met with Mark. A smaller mature CIDP was selected for the project than the one we had originally been considering. We now have a selected specimen to build the next proposal around.

Start with the actual tree

A mature palm is a particular tree, not just a species and a trunk-height figure. Looking at candidates together gives the homeowner a chance to decide what belongs in the landscape before committing to the work.

For this project, the selection changed during that visit. The next step is to confirm the revised price and the full scope for the chosen palm. Earlier figures for a larger tree should not be treated as the final price for this one.

Removal, sourcing and installation need to connect

Finding the palm is one part of the job. The existing tree still needs a removal plan, and the replacement needs a clear delivery and installation scope. I’m working with Landscape Center on that coordination so the homeowner can understand the proposal and who is responsible for each part.

SDPP’s role is palm sourcing, customer communication and project coordination. The supplier and the contractors doing the removal, transport and installation remain responsible for their work.

The record will continue as the work happens

At this stage, the nursery visit and specimen selection have happened. Removal, arrival and installation are still ahead. I’ll update this field note as those stages are completed and documented.

Once the replacement is in place, there is a new starting point for photographs, visible-condition notes and a discussion of ongoing palm care. A proposed care plan is a separate decision for the owner.

If you’re considering a mature Canary Island date palm for a San Diego County property, read about SDPP’s sourcing and replacement coordination or tell me about the palm and the site.
