I walked part of Rancho Santa Fe looking at mature Canary Island date palms, maintenance, and the local signs that South American palm weevil is being watched.

The useful part of a walk like this is the contrast. One property has a full mature crown. Nearby, another palm has lost its canopy. A UC monitoring trap sits along the route. Another CIDP has been cut into the narrow upright shape people call a hurricane cut.

A trunk with major canopy loss beside a full mature CIDP.

A UC South American palm weevil monitoring trap along the walk.

A heavily trimmed CIDP. The narrow crown makes the maintenance choice obvious.

I watch for changes in crown fullness, the newest central growth, sudden browning, and fronds that lose their normal position. Irrigation, nutrition, pruning, pests, and other stress can overlap. The point is to notice the change early enough to investigate it.
