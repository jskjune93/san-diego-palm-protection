The Albert H. Beach House is one of the homes that makes Old Escondido look like Old Escondido. The restored 1896 Queen Anne is recognizable on its own, but the mature Canary Island date palms change the entire view from the street.

They pull the eye above the roofline and give the property scale that cannot be installed in a weekend. That relationship between a mature palm and an old house is why I photograph the wider landscape, not just isolated crowns.

I am not assessing or diagnosing the palms pictured here. I am recording what they contribute to a specific historic setting while they are still part of it.
