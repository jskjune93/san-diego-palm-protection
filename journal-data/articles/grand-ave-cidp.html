I was walking through Escondido's historic district when I stopped to talk with a resident about the Canary Island date palms nearby.

His comment was blunt: “It's only a matter of time with these weevils.”

He told me that adult weevils were found after a nearby palm was removed. I did not inspect that palm or verify the dismantling findings, so I am keeping that part attributed to him. What I could photograph was the contrast around us: full mature crowns, a fresh stump, and dead palms within the same historic neighborhood.

Old Escondido has remarkable homes and remarkable palms. When one crown changes quickly, I would rather have an earlier photograph and a reason to look than wait for the answer to become a stump.
