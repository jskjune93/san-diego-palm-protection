A collection with more than one kind of palm

I want a conversation about a palm collection to start with the palms actually there. In this case, the service included Chilean wine, Canary Island date and Sylvester palms. Calling the job only a Canary Island date palm visit would leave part of the work out.

For an owner with a mixed collection, that is a useful starting point when asking about care: tell me which palms you have, what you have noticed, and what work has already been done. We can then discuss the scope of an assessment and the next steps for the collection.

The visit and the follow-through
