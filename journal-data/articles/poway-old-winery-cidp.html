I spent time walking around the Espinosa / Old Winery Road area of Poway and saw some of the strongest Canary Island Date Palm landscapes I've come across in North County San Diego.

The palms do real work in these landscapes. Large Canary Island date palms carry the scale of the houses. Queen palms, fan palms, and silver-blue specimens fill out the collection without competing for the same role.

The mature palms carry the property

A mature Canary Island date palm takes decades to develop this size and presence. Remove one and the house does not look the same.

Trimming alone is not a care plan. I would want a current photograph, a reasoned irrigation and nutrition program, and preventive treatment where the palm and site make it appropriate.

The decline is in the same neighborhood

The healthy palms were not the only thing I found. Nearby CIDPs had thinning crowns, collapsing structure, or advanced dieback.

In areas where South American Palm Weevil pressure is a concern, these early warning signs matter. By the time a mature Canary Island Date Palm shows major visual decline, intervention can become much more difficult.

These are worth getting ahead of

The Espinosa and Old Winery Road area makes the point without much explanation. The mature palms are a large part of why these properties look established.

If I owned one of these large CIDPs, I would not wait for the crown to fail before deciding whether it deserved monitoring and preventive protection.
