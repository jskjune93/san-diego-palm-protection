What I deliver

A dated starting point for your palm.

I photograph the entire palm, its crown, trunk, base, and any concern I can safely see. Those photographs give us something real to compare later.

The palm and its setting

I note where it stands, what can be accessed safely, and what prompted the visit.

Dated photographs

I take whole-palm and detail views that can be repeated during a future visit.

What I observed

I separate what I saw from what you reported, what I suspect, and what would need confirmation.

Your written report

You receive the photographs, findings, limits of the visit, and the next steps I recommend.

What it is not

Useful because its limits are explicit.

A visual assessment cannot promise tree safety, uncover hidden conditions, replace laboratory confirmation, or guarantee an outcome.

Urgent structural or life-safety concerns may require an emergency contractor or other specialist.
