Start with the property

A practical path from walkthrough to annual planning.

The first conversation is usually 15–20 minutes. A complete inventory or report is paid work, scoped after I understand the property.

1. Property walkthrough

Confirm palm count, species, access, known concerns, landscape responsibilities, and management objectives.

2. Paid palm portfolio baseline

Assign IDs, establish locations, create repeatable photographs, document visible condition, and identify priorities.

3. Stewardship plan

Set priorities for health observation, fertilization, watering or irrigation guidance, preventive protection, treatment when appropriate, and escalation.

4. Recurring stewardship

Schedule hands-on visits, appropriate care, comparable photographs, treatment and work history, and management reporting.

5. Response coordination

Organize contractor questions, removal documentation, access, loss records, sourcing, and replacement planning when required.

6. Annual planning

Prepare a concise portfolio summary for operational priorities and budgeting.
