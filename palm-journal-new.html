What We Watch For

Palm problems are often easier to address early. A palm does not need to be collapsing before it deserves attention.

  • Crown thinning or uneven growth
  • Abnormal browning or yellowing
  • Weak or distorted new spear growth
  • Visible trunk damage, wet areas, or unusual holes
  • Irrigation stress from too much or too little water
  • Nutrient issues, pests, disease, and local SAPW risk

Prevention Mindset

We believe palm protection should be proactive. By the time a valuable palm is badly declining, options may be limited and removal can be expensive.

Preventative care may include visual assessment, soil drench treatment, exterior treatment options, irrigation review, and repeat monitoring over time.

The goal is not to scare homeowners. The goal is to help them understand the condition of their palms and make smart decisions before problems become obvious.
